Why this OCR tool helps

Reading text from a photo or screenshot is often slower than copying the same content from an editable file. This extract text from image no signup tool solves that by converting images into text with OCR directly in your browser. You can use it for receipts, forms, screenshots, handwritten notes, education materials, and archived documents when you need an easy way to digitize information without installing software.

The tool is designed to be private and simple. Your image stays local to your device while the OCR engine processes it in-browser, which helps avoid uploading sensitive documents to third-party servers. It also supports multiple languages, so you can work with English, Urdu, Hindi, or Arabic content depending on your needs.

How to extract text from an image

  1. Upload the image you want to read.
  2. Select the language that matches the image content.
  3. Click Extract text and wait for OCR to finish.
  4. Copy the result or download it as a .txt file.
